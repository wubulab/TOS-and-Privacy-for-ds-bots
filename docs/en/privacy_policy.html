Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23.04.2024

This Privacy Policy describes what data may be processed by the Discord bots owned and operated by wubulab (the "bots"), for what purposes, and under what circumstances such data may be shared with third-party services. This Policy applies to all current and future bots operated by the administrator, unless a separate policy is published for a specific bot.

1. General Provisions

  1. The bots are intended to operate within the discord.gg/aruku community and its related private servers.
  2. Adding any of the bots to other servers without prior agreement with the administrator does not imply that functional features will be provided. On such servers, features may be disabled, limited, or unavailable.
  3. By using any of the bots, the user understands and agrees that certain Discord data and related technical data may be processed to the extent necessary for the bot's features to function.

2. What Data May Be Processed

Depending on the features used and the specific bot, the following categories of data may be processed.

2.1. Discord Identifiers

  • Discord user ID;
  • guild (server) ID;
  • channel ID;
  • message ID;
  • role IDs;
  • interaction IDs and other technical Discord object identifiers.

2.2. Server Configuration Data

  • role and channel settings required for the bots to function;
  • internal parameters related to schedules, events, notifications, permissions, and internal modules;
  • server-specific configuration for the bots.

2.3. Interaction Data

  • slash commands and prefix-based text commands (e.g. commands like !command);
  • button clicks, select menu choices, modal submissions, and other Discord interaction events;
  • internal responses related to the use of commands and components.

2.4. Messages and Content

Certain bots may request access to message content (Message Content Intent), but only to the extent required for a specific, explicitly triggered feature initiated by a user or authorized moderator, including:

  • activity counting (message counts) without storing the text itself, unless required by the feature;
  • processing prefix-based text commands (e.g. !) addressed to the bot;
  • moderator-only utility features that allow an authorized moderator to retrieve the author and text of a specific referenced message by its ID or link, for moderation purposes;
  • individual direct messages sent to the bot, if the user explicitly uses the corresponding feature.

The bots do not read or analyze message content in bulk or outside the context of a specific, activated feature.

2.5. Server Members Data

Certain bots may request the list of server members and their roles (Server Members Intent) in order to:

  • track role assignment and removal in real time;
  • periodically audit member roles to synchronize internal progress, achievements, or access levels.

2.6. Voice Activity and Event-Related Data

If the corresponding features are enabled, the bots may process:

  • data on users joining, leaving, and being present in voice channels;
  • data related to event activities;
  • intermediate and final results of interactive features, polls, member selections, and other event scenarios.

2.7. Application and Internal Process Data

If a user uses application/submission features or participates in internal server scenarios, the bots may process:

  • submission data;
  • comments related to such submissions;
  • review outcomes;
  • internal status markers;
  • data required to assign, remove, or control roles and access levels.

2.8. Reward and Integration Data

If the corresponding features are enabled, the bots may process data required to grant rewards or perform internal calculations via connected external APIs, including:

  • the user's Discord ID;
  • eligibility information for a reward;
  • reward amounts;
  • technical data confirming a completed or attempted payout.

2.9. Images and Attachments

If a user knowingly uses an image upload feature, the bot may:

  • retrieve the attachment link from Discord;
  • forward the image to a third-party image hosting service;
  • store the resulting link for further use within the bot's features.

2.10. Technical Logs

To ensure stability, security, and error diagnostics, the bots may process startup and runtime logs, error information, technical request parameters, command and event execution data, and other diagnostic records.

3. Purposes of Data Use

Data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • operating the bots and their modules;
  • storing and applying server configuration;
  • processing commands, buttons, menus, and other interactions;
  • supporting events, interactive features, polls, and related scenarios;
  • processing submissions, roles, and administrative/moderation scenarios;
  • granting rewards and operating connected integrations;
  • storing and displaying images within the intended functionality;
  • preventing abuse, diagnosing errors, and providing technical support;
  • developing, testing, improving, and administering the bots.

4. Where and For Whom the Bots Operate

  1. The bots are designed to operate solely within the discord.gg/aruku community ecosystem and its related private servers.
  2. Data processing and feature availability are specifically oriented toward such servers and related processes.
  3. Adding a bot to third-party servers without agreement with the administrator does not mean that features will be enabled, configured, or available.
  4. The administrator is not obligated to provide support, adaptation, or functionality for the bots on unauthorized servers.

5. Disclosure of Data to Third Parties

The bots do not sell users' personal data.

However, certain data may be shared with third-party services to the extent necessary for a specific feature, for example:

  • Discord, as the platform through which interaction occurs;
  • data storage, caching, logging, and hosting services used to operate the bots;
  • external APIs related to internal economy, images, notifications, or other features;
  • infrastructure providers hosting the bots.

6. Data Retention Period

Data may be retained:

  • for as long as necessary for the corresponding bot to function;
  • for as long as the feature for which the data was collected remains active;
  • for a reasonable period for diagnostics, security, auditing, and dispute resolution;
  • until deletion or cleanup by the administrator, if technically feasible and not contrary to operational or security obligations.

Some data may be deleted automatically, cleaned up according to internal rules, or overwritten with new values.

7. Security

The administrator takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to limit unauthorized access to data. However, absolute security of data transmission and storage over the internet cannot be guaranteed.

8. User and Server Administrator Rights

To the extent technically feasible, a user or an authorized server representative may contact the administrator to request:

  • clarification of what data is used in connection with the bot's operation;
  • deletion of specific data, where this is possible without compromising functionality, security, or legitimate operational interests;
  • discontinuation of the use of specific features, where this relates to a managed server and is available within the bot's current architecture.

Requests are reviewed by the administrator on a case-by-case basis and do not guarantee mandatory deletion of all data in every case.

9. Data of Minors

The bots operate within Discord. The user is responsible for complying with age and other requirements set by Discord and applicable server usage rules.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be changed at any time. The current version is published at a permanent link. Continued use of any of the bots after publication of an updated version constitutes acceptance of the new version.
